## Overview
ReadAssist is a lightweight AI companion built for E-ink tablets. Powered by Gemini 2.5, it analyzes screenshots, understands handwriting, and answers your reading questions without breaking your flow.

## Highlights
- **E-ink first** – UI and interactions tuned for iReader & Supernote.
- **One-tap AI** – Snap a screenshot and get instant insights.
- **Handwriting aware** – Detects highlights, notes, and doodles.
- **Floating chat** – Minimal overlay you can summon or dismiss anytime.
- **Offline friendly** – Chats saved locally; export when needed.
- **Bilingual UI** – Switch between English and Chinese on the fly.

## Supported Devices
• iReader X3 Pro and other iReader models with page-turn keys.  
• Supernote A5X2 / A6X2 (system screenshot & floating button supported).

## Quick Start
1. **Get the APK** – Grab the latest build from [Releases](https://github.com/James-Zhu-CA/ReadAssist/releases).
2. **Enable installs from unknown sources**.
3. **Launch ReadAssist** and grant overlay + accessibility permissions.
4. **Add your Gemini API key** (500 free calls/day). Get your key here: https://aistudio.google.com/welcome
5. Tap **Start Service**. You're set!

### Using ReadAssist
**iReader**  
• Settings → map *press-and-hold top page-turn key* to Screenshot.  
• Press & hold to capture; the chat pops up with the image attached.

**Supernote**  
• Method 1 – Swipe down the status bar and tap *Screenshot*.  
• Method 2 – Tap the floating button; ReadAssist screenshots automatically.

**Pro tip** – In the chat, select any text from the AI reply and long-press to copy it into the input box for a follow-up question.
